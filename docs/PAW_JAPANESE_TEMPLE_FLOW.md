# Fortune Temple: approved Chinese fortune ritual in a Japanese-inspired world
Supersedes the Japanese-only ritual contract. User explicitly retained moon-block confirmation and redraws. Mountain surroundings may remain Japanese-inspired; temple worship and architecture must be recognisably Chinese and not presented as Japanese Buddhist practice. Fictional temple, not a replica or universal religious instruction.

## Space and route
Entrance courtyard -> optional preparation -> incense offering near approach -> visible deity/altar and prayer -> adjacent fortune-stick station and clear ground for throwing blocks -> numbered slip collection counter -> optional side courtyard/garden -> exit. Suggested order, not forced tour. Do not put garden doorway behind altar or block worship aisle. Bell optional, not a prerequisite. Provide separate outdoor ventilated remembrance/joss-paper burner near garden access, away from main path and foliage; optional respectful fictional activity, not a reward mechanic. Do not burn fortune slips. Final layout must be checked against actual collision geometry before artwork production.

## Fortune gameplay contract
Pray; optional permission throw (a separate introductory custom, not counted as a candidate draw); shake tube, draw exactly one numbered stick, hold/inspect number, then throw both blocks for confirmation. One flat face and one curved face up = 圣筊, accepted. Both flat = 笑筊; both curved = 阴筊; neither accepts. On failure return stick visibly, shake and draw again, then throw again. Maximum THREE failed candidate draws per local calendar day (game adaptation, not a universal custom). Successful third draw is valid. On third failure show gentle closing message, no numbered slip issued. Save pending candidate and attempt count; no reset on back/reload/rotation/language switch. No duplicate charge or result. No forced permission retry loop. A confirmed stick gives its corresponding fixed fortune slip with a number, rank (上上签、上签、中签、下签、下下签), interpretation and optional private journal/share. Failed confirmation is not 下下签. No fear-based prediction. Retain existing technical IDs and stored records where compatible. Do not invent price or rewards.

## Visual specification
Clean portrait and landscape architecture with altar, worship aisle, exit and side access. Transparent altar furnishings, incense sticks and holder, flame/smoke, tube, numbered bamboo sticks, flat and curved block faces, numbered paper slip drawers, slip templates, garden furniture, optional outdoor burner and paper offering. Separate character kneel/bow/rise, shake/grip/pickup/return, two-block grip/release and retrieve poses. Blocks tumble locally, settle without sliding, render exactly matching stored result; no floating sticks, teleporting props or premature result overlay. Paper rank and number are live localized text, not painted. Optional burner animation restrained, no open flames in room. Ambient light, candle flicker, smoke and foliage independent. No duplicated props baked into background.

## UAT
Verify approach, hands and floor contact, accepted first/third draw, both failure face combinations, third failure, cancel mid-draw, reload, next day, language switch, orientation switch, save compatibility, garden return and exit. EN or ZH only. Preserve temple access even when daily draw limit reached. This document is design only, not an implementation claim.
