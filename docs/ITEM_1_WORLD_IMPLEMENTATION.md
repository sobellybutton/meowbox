## 3. Art and layout briefs

Use original nostalgic Japanese watercolor/gouache, weathered timber, natural soft light, believable material and depth. No stylized flat geometry, embedded text/characters or fake pseudo-isometric floors. Use consistent character identity, common world geography and lighting logic while giving each district its own recognizable architecture and palette. Portrait-first (proposed canvas 1000 x 1700–1900 logical units; actual dimensions chosen after layout/art alignment); desktop gets an independently composed landscape environment or full portrait playfield with intentional painted side treatment, never stretching/cropping away doors. Each painting must have uncluttered sign zones, clear near-to-far walkable path, door thresholds, readable landmarks and foreground occlusion. Define geometry before rendering final art; trace on the actual produced image and inspect it at runtime. Proposed near-to-far order follows existing source anchors.

Fortune Valley / 解忧谷: matching spring or coherent seasonal entrance and foothill village, stone stairs/paving, moss, timber, red accents, quiet mountain mist. Spawn at near gate, facing uphill; tarot on near left, destiny on right further along, one Fortune Temple on left mid-slope with visible entrance, Wishing Garden on right further uphill, Railway Station at far end. Do not allow walking onto Mt Fuji, rooftops, railings, lantern plinths, flowerbeds, walls or sky. Use wider stair landings where paths branch. Temple approach is inside temple doorway, not duplicated outside. Each entrance must be independently recognizable.

Lazy Island / existing runtime Chinese name 咸鱼岛: breezy coastal village with weathered wood, sea green, sandy stone and sheltered waterfront. Near Worry-Free Pier with a dry connected release courtyard (no fishing as standalone game), tea room further up on right and park upper right. Keep a physical shoreline barrier and safe shore path; do not walk on water or across mooring furniture. The office comedy is inside Tea Room, not an Office façade with clickable entry. Use a separate safe visitor waiting area near tea room, not in front of its plaque.

Munch & Play / 吃喝玩乐街: retro market lane, warm shop windows, timber/stone, subtle awnings, old arcade architecture. Near bubble tea counter/shop, kitchen on left, arcade up right, souvenir shop up left. Preserve boba entry until approved consolidation with verified migration. Distinct silhouettes for all active doors. Outdoor shop displays are decorative and outside navigable path. No character walking through a stall, food counter or sign. Reserve enough clear pavement for passing visitors.

Discovery Station / 奇遇驿站: old observatory/library setting, indigo, muted brass, paper lanterns, garden/path. Compatibility Café near right, Parallel Lives on left further up, Personality Lab at upper right. Avoid making all buildings look like the temple. Observatory dome is scenery, not a walkable platform or extra destination. No duplicate astrology or tarot door. Keep actual building entrances legible at phone scale.

## 4. New runtime staging data (proposed implementation schema)

One validated versioned district manifest replaces scattered geometry values for new art. Store each district independently: asset path, original width/height, logical width/height, normalized-to-world transform, spawn {foot, facing}, transport arrivals, walkable floor polygons or connected nodes/edges (with widths and clearance), obstacle polygons, foreground occlusion regions, doors {id, threshold, approach, facing, plaqueAnchor, signSafeZone, returnFoot, returnFacing}, depth/scale references, camera limits, NPC lanes/pause anchors and no-standing regions, weather layer anchors and asset readiness. Include stable IDs and no new save keys without migration. All coordinates derive from the same artwork scale; never map to new art by copying old coordinates. Use a manifest switch only after each new painting is complete and validated. If one district is missing final art, leave that district explicitly unfinished rather than relabel old art. Proposed path assets/visual/painted/worlds/<district>/ with scene.webp, foreground.webp (alpha) and layout.json; optional landscape composition with its own validated transform. Match current asset/build conventions or document migration. No paint-over of existing approved art without justification.

Walkability: trace the full walkable ground and all branch approach routes at final artwork dimensions. Compute route on ground plane with avatar-foot clearance. Every route segment must be safe, not merely endpoints or a broad y-band. Project invalid destination to a reachable nearby ground point or ignore with immediate subtle feedback. Use the same polygon/graph for taps, keyboard, joystick, swipes, automatic approach, NPCs and return. One tap to approach then enter automatically at threshold; cancel cleanly on new movement/overlay, never invoke a room by timeout alone. Exit through the same doorway to a registered safe returnFoot facing outward, preserving map camera and context. Use foot-based sorting, scale by ground depth, foreground masks only where actually occluding. Avoid lateral camera jitter and rendering coordinates different from real coordinates.

Signs: physically anchored to painted façade above/outside pedestrian heads, compact bilingual localized single-language text with clear contrast, minimum 44 CSS px target using unobtrusive larger hit area; no duplicate floating shortcut or long descriptive label. One camera transform for canvas and sign positions; update transforms in same render frame, only remeasure text on layout/language/font changes. Existing syncSigns has 32ms position throttle and dynamically shifts/hides labels to avoid UI; evaluate this in playback rather than assuming it fixes jitter. Preserve a stable plaque anchor, and if offscreen leave it offscreen instead of clamping multiple houses to same place. Route NPCs away from label/door waiting areas; if actor would overlap a label use layout/depth/placement that does not cover the actor face or plaque. Ambient dialogue moves or waits; never blocks a door, HUD or touch target. No permanent icons floating above characters.

Camera: follow foot position smoothly with a dead zone; bounds based on new art, no sudden reset except intentional arrival. Respect safe areas and keyboard, both portrait and landscape. Keep player and immediate path readable with room for upcoming doors. Show enough vertical space to orient, scroll upward naturally. Desktop must remain usable without shrinking characters into tiny figures. No extra full-screen menu when crossing a door. Keep four existing dock functions intact; redesign dock appearance only where necessary for nonoverlap with item 1, full redesign is a later separate item.
