# Physical travel journal and dock design
Owner clarification, 28 November 2026. Mandatory Phase 1 design direction, under UX-01 and UX-COHESION-01; verification under QA-PLAYER-LOOP-01 and QA-01. This elaborates the existing four destinations, not a new workstream or additional menu.

## Art direction and actual interaction
The player opens a real illustrated personal journal carried in the world. One tap on a dock destination raises the closed painted book, opens its hinged cover and turns directly to the intended chapter. Show a continuous, visually convincing physical book with spine, page block, binding, ribbon or bookmark, paper texture, hand-painted illustrations and inked typography. Animate the actual illustrated cover and page geometry, not the whole web panel tilting or fading. Closing reverses the book action and returns to exactly the prior player position; no extra confirmation, duplicate navigation, opaque transition stall or artificial wait. Keep ambient world state and audio continuous. Respect reduced motion; preserve immediate interaction, drafts, scroll/selection, saved progress, touch and keyboard accessibility. Do not fabricate a book from a paper wallpaper and rectangles and call it finished.

## Four chapters of one volume
- Explore: illustrated travel passport, map spreads, route and destination art, real stamps, visited places and travel memories. Page corner or swipe turns to another district; one canonical departure action. No dashboard cards.
- Today: handwritten diary with date and daily tasks, optional luck, lunar calendar and private gratitude written as entries, not separate apps. Entries open or turn within the book.
- Town Hall: village gazette, illustrated local notices (ten rotating notices), resident stories, tea chat and little joys. Read, reply and return within the same book; no repeated hero banners or full-scene wallpaper under text.
- My Room: personal scrapbook with belongings, collected stamps, keepsakes and furnishings; keep the actual room and furniture interactions physically playable in the illustrated scene. The scrapbook is a purposeful object, not a substitute for the room.

Mobile: readable single-page layout, full viewport, binding and page edges remain visible, 44px targets, comfortable line lengths; preserve safe areas. Desktop: an actual open spread with two coordinated pages, not merely a vertical line behind a wide website. Page turn direction follows previous/next; real content remains live and selectable. Use existing world typography, palette, lighting and materials; bilingual EN/ZH with no technical labels or generic web-style boxes.
