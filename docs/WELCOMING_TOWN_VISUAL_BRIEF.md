## First impression
A small, warm town people want to revisit during a break. Each doorway leads to a recognisable place, not a floating image/card. Entry view shows the resident and actual playable station, with a short greeting and clear invitation; player approaches before camera moves closer. No blocking dialogue or repetitive onboarding. A familiar resident recognises return visits, with gentle humour and optional exit. Never imply simulated guests are real people.

## Scene production
Every retained activity gets a native portrait and desktop composition with grounded work surface, independent interactive sprites, foreground occlusion, contact shadows, a safe player lane and guest lane. No baked UI text, faces, clickable objects or labels. Use warm illustrated paper, painted signage, quiet textures and localised dialogue with a tail anchored to the speaker, positioned out of hands/controls. One visual focus at a time. No huge floating activity boards covering the room. Each room has its own palette and recognizable silhouette. Preserve accessible 44px targets.

## Residents
Use separate identity sheets with distinct faces, noses, brows, hair, body shapes, age cues, clothing, height and silhouettes. Resident identity stays consistent in all rooms and directions. Master Yue: silver hair, round spectacles, soft moss linen robe, folding fan, amused expression; seated, book-reading, tea-pouring, pointing, idle and walking poses. Grandma: rounded, floral apron; grumpy villain-hitting Auntie: bun, stern brows, slipper, seated pose, distinct from Grandma and Mei Auntie. Mei Auntie: curls, patterned blouse, tote; temple keeper: slender, calm, practical robes; Wang Laoban: rolled sleeves, tired smile; salaryman: untidy hair, crooked tie, slouch. Tourists, students, couples and occasional eccentric cameos must not share resident faces. Preserve player-chosen identity and separate NPC sprite sheets from environment art.
Foot-pivot sorting, natural strides, staggered speed and asynchronous visits, safe lanes; no same-face clones or NPC blocking activity target, UI or player. Welcome without excessive chatter. Distinguish actual real community posts from scripted visitors.
