# Topical satire editorial workflow

Pawradise is fictional. The optional same-origin assets/content/pawradise-topical.json is an editorial index, not a news reader. Only reviewed everyday topics, a verified HTTPS source URL, publication/review/expiry dates and an existing prewritten template ID can influence the fictional Gazette. No raw headline, article, source attribution, tracking request to the publisher or publisher-provided text is rendered. Expiry is exclusive in Singapore local time, maximum seven days after publication. Invalid or unavailable records fall back to evergreen satire. No actual price or product claim is made by the joke.

First record: 23 November 2026 SingStat CPI release, cross-check MAS, reviewed 26 November. General consumer-price discussion inspired biscuit packaging satire; source does NOT assert shrinkflation or a particular seller's conduct. Remove/replace the entry after expiry. Sources remain internal development metadata in this public repository; not confidential. Future daily retrieval/editorial workflow not configured. Do not enable publisher RSS scraping or syndication without reviewing the source terms (CNA RSS terms limit use to personal/non-commercial use, BBC feeds have their own terms). Keep only authorized public facts and original fictional adaptation. No disaster, victim, health, criminal allegation, political attack, real celebrity scandal or fabricated current event.
